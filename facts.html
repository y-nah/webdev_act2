🐾 All About Cats: The Mystery, Mischief & Magic of Our Feline Friends

🐱 Purr-fect Beginnings

Cats weren’t always house companions. In ancient Egypt, they were worshipped as sacred beings. Sailors once kept them on ships for good luck (and pest control). Today, they rule our homes, demanding cuddles… on their terms. 😜

🐾 Meet the Breeds

Not all cats are created equal! Here are a few favorites:

Persian The ultimate fluffy couch potato.
Siamese Elegant, chatty, and full of personality.
Maine Coon Gentle giants with dog-like friendliness.
Sphynx Bald but bold (and surprisingly warm to cuddle!).
